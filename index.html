How It Works

The walk: Follow clues across ~10 stops from near Waterloo through central London — riddles, what you see around you, and bite-sized history. Go at your own speed.

The letter (optional): Add a message in the booking form, or skip it if you only want the mystery. Your text feeds into the letter from 1800 revealed at the finish — proposals, thanks, gifts, or team shout-outs.

Who plays: Anyone — solo, couples, friends, family, or colleagues. One booking; you choose gift or join-in, and who the message is for.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the treasure hunt take?
Most players complete the journey in 1-2 hours. You can take breaks and go at your own pace.
Do I need to book in advance?
Yes. Book online to receive your game link. The game is available 15 minutes before your chosen slot.
Can I play with a group?
Absolutely! The game is designed for solo players, couples, families, or groups. You'll receive a unique link to play together.
Is it suitable for children?
Yes. The puzzles and historical content are family-friendly. Younger children may need adult help with some clues.
I only want the outdoor mystery — do I have to add a message?
No. The personal message is optional. You can book purely for the clues, riddles, walk, and story.
Can I book mainly to tell someone something important (e.g. proposal or thank-you)?
Yes — that’s exactly what the booking form is for. You write what you want them to read; we weave it into the letter from 1800 revealed at the end. You can play with them or gift the slot so they go on their own.
Can I give this as a gift?
Yes. Book a slot for someone else, add a message if you like, and share the game link. Great for birthdays, anniversaries, colleagues, family days, or a last-minute surprise.
How do player(s) get their personalised lost letter from 1800?
When you book, you can add a personal message, dedication, or surprise for the player(s).
How does the personalised letter work?
When you reach the final stage of the game, your full personalised letter—written as if from 1800 will be delivered to the player(s). The letter is crafted from your words, woven into our time-travel story.
Where does the outdoor treasure hunt start?
Your route begins near Waterloo Station and winds through central London. You'll follow clues to roughly ten stops — comfortable shoes and a charged phone are all you need.
Is this a good summer activity in London?
Yes. It's designed as an outdoor walking experience you can enjoy in daylight — perfect for warmer months, visitors, and anyone who wants a fun day out with a story.
